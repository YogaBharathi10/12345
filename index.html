🔍 Why JustDial Works for HYTRIX High Volume

JustDial is India's largest local business directory. Most listings are small businesses with no website — which is exactly your target. You can filter by city, category, and phone number availability.

✅ What you'll find
  • Business name & owner's phone number
  • Category (clinic, gym, restaurant…)
  • Address & locality
  • Ratings (helps prioritise serious businesses)
  • Website status — many show "No Website"
⚡ Why it's fast
  • Hundreds of listings per category per city
  • Phone numbers directly visible (WhatsApp)
  • No login required for basic browsing
  • Filter by "No Website" to pre-qualify leads
  • Free to use — no paid tools needed
🪜 Step-by-Step Hunting Process Follow This Order
1
Go to JustDial.com
Open justdial.com in your browser. No account needed. Use desktop for easiest data access.
2
Enter your target category + city
In the search bar, type the business type and your target city. Use the search queries below. Start with your own city (Vellore / Chennai) to build a local portfolio.
3
Filter for businesses without a website
On the results page, look at each listing. Those that show No Website Listed or no URL are your primary targets. Some listings show a broken/basic website — still worth approaching.
4
Check their rating & reviews
Target businesses with 3.5★ or higher and at least 10 reviews. They're established, profitable, and care about their reputation — making them more likely to invest in a website.
5
Copy their phone number
Click "View Number" to reveal the full number. Save it with the business name in a spreadsheet. This number is your WhatsApp outreach contact. Verify on Truecaller to confirm it's active.
6
Log 20–30 leads per session
Set a target: collect 20–30 leads in one sitting (30–40 mins). Log: Business Name | Owner Name (if shown) | Phone | Category | City | Has Website? in a Google Sheet.
7
Send messages using the Contact Toolkit
Open your HYTRIX Contact Toolkit, pick the matching niche template, enter the business name, copy the WhatsApp message, and send. Simple.

🗺️ Why Google Maps is a Gold Mine Best Quality Leads

Google Maps shows real, active businesses that customers are already searching for. You can instantly see if they have a website, check their reviews, and sometimes find their email. These are warmer leads than cold directories.

✅ What you can find
  • Business name, address & phone number
  • Website link (or clear "No website" signal)
  • Reviews & ratings (quality filter)
  • Business hours & photos
  • Sometimes: Email, WhatsApp, social links
🎯 The #1 signal to look for
  • No website listed → instant target
  • Website says "under construction"
  • Website is on free platforms (Wix, Blogspot)
  • Website looks 5+ years old / not mobile-friendly
  • All their info comes only from Google Maps
🪜 Step-by-Step Hunting Process Follow This Order
1
Open Google Maps on desktop
Go to maps.google.com. Desktop gives you more space to scan the sidebar listing panel while seeing the map.
2
Search your target niche + city
Type queries like dental clinic Vellore or gym in Ranipet. The left sidebar will populate with listings. You'll see 20–60+ results per search.
3
Scan listings quickly for website status
Click each listing. If the panel shows no website link — that's your lead. If there's a website, click it quickly to check if it's outdated, broken, or not mobile-friendly.
4
Check rating and review count
Only approach businesses with 3.8★+ and 15+ reviews. Fewer reviews means the business might be new or struggling — harder to convert.
5
Get the phone number & check for email
Phone number is usually visible. Scroll down in the listing panel — sometimes the business has linked their website or Facebook where you can find an email. Email outreach converts better for professional niches (CAs, lawyers).
6
Use Google Maps URLs to navigate faster
Bookmark the search URL format: maps.google.com/search/[category]+[city] — you can swap categories quickly without retyping. Pan the map to explore neighboring towns and areas.
7
Log leads & send outreach
Add to your Google Sheet. Use the Contact Toolkit's WhatsApp template for local businesses, Email template for professionals. Mention their Google Maps presence — it shows you did your research.
⚡ Speed-Hunting with Google Maps Advanced Tactics
🖱️ Keyboard shortcut flow
Click a listing → check for website → press Esc to go back → click next. With practice you can scan 60 businesses in 20 minutes.
📍 Explore nearby towns
Search the same niche in Katpadi, Ranipet, Ambur, Tirupathur — less competition, same services needed. Small-town businesses are easier to close.
📸 Profile photo check
Businesses with many photos and no website are perfect targets — they're digitally active but don't have a proper web presence. High conversion potential.
🔗 Find their Instagram
If Google Maps shows no website but links an Instagram or Facebook — check if they have a following. Big following + no website = very hot lead for HYTRIX.
📸 Why Instagram Leads are Different Warm Outreach

Instagram leads are warmer than directory leads — these businesses are already marketing themselves visually, which means they care about their image. They're more receptive to a web design pitch. The tell-tale sign: big following + no website link in bio.

🎯 The perfect Instagram lead profile
  • 500–10,000 followers (local/micro business)
  • Posts regularly (3+ times/week)
  • No website in bio — just phone or WhatsApp
  • Gets comments & engagement on posts
  • Services business (beauty, food, fitness, etc.)
🚫 Skip these accounts
  • Personal accounts (not a business)
  • Under 200 followers (not serious enough)
  • Already has a professional website linked
  • Inactive — last post was 2+ months ago
  • Large brands or franchises
🪜 Step-by-Step Hunting Process Follow This Order
1
Search Instagram hashtags for your niche + city
In Instagram search, try hashtags like #VelloreClinic, #ChennaiGym, #ChennaiBakery. Top posts and Recent posts both show real business accounts actively using Instagram.
2
Check the account profile
Click on a promising post → go to the account. Check: Is it a business? Do they have followers? Is there a website in the bio? If no website — this is your lead.
3
Check their bio for contact info
Most local businesses put their WhatsApp number or phone number in the bio. Some have a Linktree or similar — click it to check if they have a real site or just social links.
4
Look at their posts & engagement
Good engagement (comments, saves, shares relative to followers) = active business. Scan 3–4 recent posts. If they're posting consistently, they're invested in growth — perfect for your pitch.
5
Choose your outreach method
WhatsApp: If number is in bio, send the Contact Toolkit WA message — mention their Instagram content specifically. Instagram DM: If no number, send a DM (shorter, casual tone). Email: Some accounts have email in bio — use the email template.
6
Personalise your message using their content
Before sending, note something specific: "I saw your reel about your new smoothie menu" or "Your transformation posts are great." This one-line personalisation dramatically increases reply rates.
💡 Key advantage: When you reach out on WhatsApp and mention their Instagram, they know you've done research. It signals that you're a real professional, not a bot — which makes the free mockup offer much more compelling.
